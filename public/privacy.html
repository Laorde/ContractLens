Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026
We've written this in plain English. We've left out sections we're not certain about — such as specific regional compliance details (e.g. full GDPR or CCPA frameworks) — rather than include language we can't stand behind. If you have specific privacy requirements, please contact us directly.

1. What We Collect

Account information: When you sign up, we collect your email address. This is stored securely via Supabase.

Contract content: When you upload or paste a contract for analysis, that content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the analysis. It is not stored permanently on our servers after the analysis is returned.

Usage data: We track how many scans you've used in a given month to enforce plan limits. This is stored in your profile in our database.

Payment information: Payments are handled entirely by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details. We store only your Stripe customer ID and subscription status.

2. What We Don't Do

— We do not sell your data to third parties
— We do not use your contract content to train AI models
— We do not share your personal information with advertisers

3. Third-Party Services We Use

Supabase — stores your account and usage data. Supabase Privacy Policy

Stripe — processes payments. Stripe Privacy Policy

Anthropic — powers the contract analysis via their Claude API. Your contract content is sent to Anthropic's API to generate analysis. Anthropic Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention

Your account data (email, plan, usage count) is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is deleted from our database. Contract content you submit for analysis is not stored after the analysis is returned to you.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication via Supabase, and we do not store sensitive payment data. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. You can update your email address through your account settings.

If you are located in the EU or UK and have specific GDPR-related requests, please contact us directly and we will do our best to assist. We have not included a full GDPR compliance section here as we are not certain of all applicable requirements for our specific situation.

7. Cookies

We use only the cookies necessary to keep you logged in (session cookies via Supabase). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered users by email if there are significant changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

9. Contact

If you have any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at: hello@contractlens.app
